Master's Thesis: Digital Product Passport Framework with PLM/ERP Integration

TU Berlin · M.Sc. Global Production Engineering
Digital Product PassportPLMERPSustainabilityTraceabilitySystems Integration

A framework for a Digital Product Passport (DPP) and how it connects to PLM and ERP systems, so product lifecycle and sustainability data can be captured once and shared across the value chain.

Problem
Upcoming EU product passport requirements need lifecycle data that today sits scattered across PLM, ERP and supplier systems.
Approach
Defined DPP data requirements and mapped them onto PLM and ERP system landscapes and data flows.
Outcome
An integration framework for DPP adoption in manufacturing companies. Full thesis available on request.

Case Study: Quality System & Quality Gates for an Aerospace Component Program

Excellence Technologies · Moog Controls FM306 · AS9100

AS9100APQPControl PlansQuality Gates8D / 5-WhyLeanTraining

Built the quality backbone for a high-precision aerospace supply program: QMS framework, control plans and enforced quality gates.

Problem
New aerospace component launches with recurring non-conformances, customer complaints and late deliveries.
Approach
APQP-aligned NPI quality planning, control plans, gated production with NC escalation, 8D/5-Why on failures, lean tools (5S, Kaizen, error-proofing) and operator training.

SCAR8DFMEAMSA / Gauge R&RSPC (Cp, Cpk)CMMGD&T

Turned a backlog of supplier quality issues into a structured SCAR process, and qualified new suppliers with measurement-backed first-article validation.

Problem
High volume of repeat supplier defects and new suppliers that had to be qualified on schedule.
Approach
End-to-end SCAR workflow with 8D, FMEA and 5-Why; supplier KPIs and containment; line trials, CMM first-article inspection, MSA and Cp/Cpk capability studies.
